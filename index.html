Our Maison

Born from
Obsession

Founded in Geneva in 1994, AURVM was born from a single obsession: to push the boundaries of what is technically and aesthetically possible in watchmaking. Every component — from the nano-machined carbon composite cases to the hand-finished tourbillon cages — is engineered with the same philosophy that drives championship racing teams.

Each timepiece takes between 6 and 18 months to complete. We produce fewer than 850 watches per year. Not by limitation — by choice.

RM 011–03
Flyback Chronograph

A masterpiece of mechanical complication, the RM 011-03 combines a flyback chronograph with annual calendar and hours and minutes functions in a barely-there 40g titanium and Carbon TPT® case.

Dimensions 50mm × 40mm
